About Me

I am an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence Convergence at Ewha Womans University (advised by Prof. Hyunsoo Cho, LKI Lab). I am currently a visiting researcher in the Digital Convergence Research Department at ETRI (Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute), where I work on reasoning LLMs for clinical decision support. My research focuses on training and adapting large language models, particularly knowledge distillation, supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, retrieval-augmented generation, and LLM-based agents. I also work on domain-specific LLMs for medical applications. I am interested in building LLMs that are efficient and reliable enough to be trusted in real-world, high-stakes domains.
