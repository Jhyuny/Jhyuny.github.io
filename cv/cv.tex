\section{Res\color{black}earch Interests}
\footnotesize
Training and adapting large language models, with a focus on knowledge distillation and post-training with
reinforcement learning, as well as retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and LLM-based agents.
Research interests include building efficient and reliable LLMs and applying them to domain-specific problems,
particularly clinical reasoning and decision support in medical AI.

\section{Res\color{black}earch Experience}
\cventry{Visiting Researcher, ETRI}{2026.01–Present}{%
  Digital Convergence Research Department, Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute\par
  Designed clinician-guided, step-by-step reasoning structures for LLMs on chronic kidney disease patient data,
  from patient-state assessment and differential diagnosis to treatment planning, and built the corresponding training data.\par
  Trained models via supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, comparing medical-domain and general-purpose LLMs
  across loss functions and tuning strategies; currently designing evaluation protocols for reasoning reliability.}
